What to send

  • Floor plans and facade elevations with dimensions required
  • Exterior materials: cladding, roof, windows and doors, with links required
  • Site plan: the plot, paving, fences and planting ideas optional

Don't worry if something is missing — I'll guide you. CAD, SketchUp, PDF or hand drawings all work.

Questions

Perspective or elevation: which do I need?

A perspective exterior shows the house in its setting, the way people will see it from the street or the garden. An elevation or section shows one side flat and to scale, with materials, shadows and planting: good for comparing facade options and for presentation boards.

Do you draw sections too?

Yes. A presentation section through the building, with materials, shadows and planting, costs the same as an elevation: `$170.

Can the exterior match my interior views?

Yes. The exterior and the interiors can come from the same 3D model, like in the From exterior to interior project, so windows, materials and proportions match.

What file will I get?

A high-resolution image at A4, 450 dpi (about 5,260 × 3,720 px), in CMYK or RGB (your choice), sharp enough for print and large screens.

How fast is it?

Up to 2 days for one view, after the 3D model (up to 1 day). Need it sooner? Express is twice as fast for +50%.
