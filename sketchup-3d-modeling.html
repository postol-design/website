Questions

White or textured model: which do I need?

A white model is enough to check the layout, proportions and furniture placement, or if you will add materials yourself. A textured model shows the room with your actual furniture, finishes, decor and lighting, ready to show a client.

Can I change the model myself?

Yes. It is a regular SketchUp file: turn it, add your own views, move or swap furniture and change materials.

Which SketchUp version do I need?

The file opens in current versions of SketchUp on desktop, web and iPad. If you work in an older version, tell me before we start.

Do you model houses and sites too?

On request. Send the drawings and I will quote the exterior or site model individually.

How fast is it?

One room in up to 1 day, 2–4 rooms in up to 2 days. Need it sooner? Express is twice as fast for +50%.

Have a project in mind?

Send a brief or just describe your project, and I'll recommend the right visualization format.
