[ The person behind the visualization ]

I draw what the designer has already imagined, and see it the way you do

I'm Svetlana Postol, an interior and architectural visualization artist. I work remotely from Ukraine with interior designers and architects worldwide, most often in the US, UK, Canada and Australia.

I turn your plans, drawings and 3D models into images your client understands at first glance and approves faster. I don't invent anything for you: your intent, proportions and materials stay exactly as they are in the project.

I came to visualization from design, not from 3D. In 2009 I started as a graphic designer and spent 14 years on print, branding and web design. That's 14 years of working with clients: I know what revisions, tight deadlines and a client who "doesn't quite know what they want yet" look like.

I've always drawn alongside: watercolor, botanical art and landscapes first, then digital painting. That's where my eye for composition, color and light comes from: it's what makes a render sell the design, not just show it.

At the end of 2023 I moved fully into interior and architectural visualization. Today every view starts with a precise 3D model in ArchiCAD and SketchUp and is finished by hand in Procreate.

My goal is to help interior designers and architects present their concepts clearly, so clients approve faster and revisions drop. The result isn't just a nice image. It's a decision-making tool that gets your client to "yes".

[ What I do ]

Every view from one model

Interior and exterior renderings, 2D and 3D floor plans, axonometric views, site and landscape plans, elevations and sections. Every view is built from one 3D model, so proportions, materials and light match from the first image to the last.

See services & pricing

How every view is made

  1. 01A precise 3D modelBuilt in ArchiCAD and SketchUp from your plans and real measurements. Proportions, materials and lighting are checked before a single stroke is drawn.
  2. 02Finished by handI bring the view to life in Procreate: light, texture and mood. No AI image generators and no stock renders.
  3. 03Ready to presentA visual your client understands in seconds, delivered as A4 at 450 dpi, in CMYK or RGB (your choice) for print and screen.
