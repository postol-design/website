Upper floor of a family home

Scott · BYO Floor Plan · Australia

01

Brief and objective

Four views of the upper floor of a family home designed by BYO Floor Plan: the hallway with its bar niche and lit shelving, the master en-suite, the office and the walk-in robe. The designer built the 3D model and set the camera angles himself; my part was the rendering, following his specification room by room.

The designer sent his own 3D model with the four angles already set, and a render brief for each room: limestone-look tumbled tiles, rural oak, V-joint wall lining, textured lime paint, tapware, the freestanding bath, wall lights and joinery details. The task was to show the client how these finishes work together before anything was ordered.

Have your own 3D model? Send it with your camera angles and I’ll render from it. If something in the model needs fixing or finishing, I’ll adjust it before rendering.

My role
Rendering the four views from the designer’s own 3D model and camera angles, finished by hand in Procreate
