What to send

  • Floor plan with furniture layout and dimensions required
  • Materials, photo references, furniture, lighting and decor: everything about the project required
  • Links to the materials and furniture required
  • Room elevations, if you have them optional

Don't worry if something is missing — I'll guide you. CAD, SketchUp, PDF or hand drawings all work.

Questions

Can you work from my own 3D model?

Yes. Send your SketchUp or ArchiCAD model and the angles you like, and I'll finish the views by hand. That's how the Upper floor of a family home project was made.

What is an extended view?

One view that covers a main room plus a second zone: a kitchen with the dining or living area, or a two-level space with part of the upper floor. It costs `$250 instead of `$210.

Do you render commercial spaces?

Yes: salons, offices, cafés, restaurants and other commercial interiors, `$250 per view. Send the plan and the materials, the same way as for a home.

What file will I get?

A high-resolution image at A4, 450 dpi (about 5,260 × 3,720 px), in CMYK or RGB (your choice), sharp enough for print and large screens.

How fast is it?

Up to 2 days for one view, after the 3D model (up to 1 day). Need it sooner? Express is twice as fast for +50%.

Have a project in mind?

Send a brief or just describe your project, and I'll recommend the right visualization format.
