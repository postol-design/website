  • 02TimingThe 3D model takes up to 1 day. One view is ready in up to 3 days; each additional angle of the same room adds 1 day. Time to review your brief isn't counted.
  • 03Express when you need itTwice as fast for +50% of the project price — e.g. $210 → $315.
  • 042 revision rounds includedOne at the 3D model stage, one on the final view. Further edits are paid and agreed in advance. The chosen angle can't change after approval.
  • 05Simple paymentPayPal: 50% before we start, 50% after you approve the angle and before final rendering. Another method if PayPal doesn't suit you.
  • 06Publication & NDAI may show the project in my portfolio and Instagram, later (1 week, 2 months, 6 months) or without crediting you. If it can't be published at all, the price increases by 40%. NDA on request.
  • 07FilesA high-resolution image at A4, 450 dpi (about 5,260 × 3,720 px), ready for print and presentations.

[ How I work ]

How a project runs

  1. 01Send your projectFloor plan with furniture and dimensions, plus materials, furniture, lighting and decor with links. Elevations if you have them.
  2. 02Quote & 50% depositI confirm the scope, the price and the start date. You pay half to book the slot.
  3. 033D model & viewI build the model from your dimensions and you choose the view. Revision round one, then the remaining 50%.
  4. 04Final viewI finish the view by hand over the approved model, following your materials and lighting.
  5. 05Revisions & filesRevision round two, then the final files: A4 at 450 dpi, ready for print and presentations.

What to send

  • Floor plan with furniture layout and dimensions required
  • Materials, photo references, furniture, lighting and decor: everything about the project required
  • Links to the materials and furniture required
  • Room elevations, if you have them optional

Don't worry if something is missing — I'll guide you. CAD, SketchUp, PDF or hand drawings all work.
